*This policy applies to the website ajrrn.org and its language versions (fr.ajrrn.org, es.ajrrn.org, ar.ajrrn.org). It does not cover the network's research activities, which are governed by separate research ethics protocols and data management plans.*

## Summary

This website is designed to collect as little information about you as possible.

- We do not use cookies.
- We do not use analytics, tracking or advertising services.
- We do not embed content from social media or other third‑party services.
- We do not ask you to create an account.

## What is collected when you visit

The site is a set of static pages served through [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/), which hosts the site and protects it from abuse. Like any web server, Cloudflare processes basic technical information about each request (such as your IP address, the page requested, your browser type and the time of the request) in order to deliver the page and to detect malicious traffic. Cloudflare's processing of this information is described in its [privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). We may see aggregate statistics about visits (for example, the number of requests per day and per country); we do not see or keep records that identify individual visitors.

Font files and images are served from this website itself, not from third‑party servers.

## Information you send us

If you email us, we will keep your message and use your contact details only to reply to you and to deal with your enquiry. If you send us content for publication (for example, for a news item or an event), we will keep it for as long as it is published.

## Links to other sites

This site links to other websites — for example, those of partner organizations, publishers and event registration pages. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

## Translations

Versions of this site in French, Spanish and Arabic are produced from the English text using generative AI and reviewed by our team. The English version is authoritative in the event of a discrepancy.
