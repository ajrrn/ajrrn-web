This report documents consultations undertaken by the Refugee Law Lab with refugee advocacy communities in Canada in response to Bill C‑2, the *Strong Borders Act*, while the AJRRN was being established. It gives an overview of the bill and its impacts on immigration and refugee law, outlines the critiques made by advocacy and human rights organizations, describes the consultation objectives and methods, presents the key themes that emerged, and proposes an action plan for the Refugee Law Lab — including priorities for the network’s research — together with recommendations for other university‑based researchers.
