The [Refugee Law Lab Portal](https://rllp.ca/) presents legal analytics about Canadian refugee decisions, using data compiled from the Federal Court and the Immigration and Refugee Board (IRB). It is an example of the rights‑enhancing technology the network builds: open, data‑driven transparency about how refugee cases are decided, to support claimants, their counsel, researchers and decision‑makers.

The portal has three sections:

- **IRB — Refugee Protection Division:** refugee claim recognition rates by Board Member, country of origin and year, from data obtained by the Refugee Law Lab through access‑to‑information requests and a data‑sharing agreement, which reveal large disparities in recognition rates across decision‑makers.
- **IRB — Refugee Appeal Division:** appeal allowal rates across Board Members for appeals brought by claimants and decided on the merits.
- **Federal Court:** outcomes of applications for leave and for judicial review of refugee determinations.

The portal is an academic research project of the Refugee Law Lab (York University), led by Sean Rehaag. It uses automated processes; its data and statistics are preliminary information intended to prompt further research and discussion, and may be outdated or inaccurate.
