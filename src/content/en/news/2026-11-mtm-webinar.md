The AJRRN Event Series opens with a webinar with our partner, the <a href="https://www.migrationtechmonitor.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Migration and Technology Monitor</a> (MTM), a global community of experts-on-the-move critiquing and developing the technologies used to manage migration. It asks what it means for people with lived experience of migration to drive the conversation on technology at the border.

Three MTM fellows will speak: Aqila Abdelkarim Ali (2025), a medical and mental health professional based in Somaliland; Grace Gichanga (2024), a lawyer and the founder of Wasafiri Foundation in South Africa; and Wael Qarssifi (2023), a journalist from Syria now living in Germany. They will be in conversation with Petra Molnar, Associate Director of the Refugee Law Lab.

**Tuesday, November 24, 2026, 12:00–1:00 p.m. EST (Toronto), online via Zoom.** The session is free and open to everyone.
