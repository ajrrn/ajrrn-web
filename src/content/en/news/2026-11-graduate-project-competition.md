summary: The network is funding four graduate-led research projects on technology, borders and refugee rights, with $10,000 CAD for each. Applications close December 1, 2026.
---
The Algorithmic Justice for Refugees Research Network is inviting graduate students and postdoctoral fellows, in Canada or abroad, to apply for four awards of $10,000 CAD. Each award funds one graduate-led research project, published as an open access journal article.

The four projects look at how Global Majority data is drawn into Global North border control, which border technologies the Canadian government buys and who builds them, what rights-enhancing technologies already exist for refugees and displaced people, and how large language models treat refugees and migrants.

Applicants work with an academic supervisor of their choice and are the lead author of the resulting article. **Applications close December 1, 2026, 11:59 pm Toronto time.**
