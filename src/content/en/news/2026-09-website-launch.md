The Algorithmic Justice for Refugees Research Network (AJRRN) has launched its website. The site will be the home for announcements, events, publications and opportunities from the network, and is available in English, French, Spanish and Arabic.

Over the coming months we will add information about the network's people, partners and first projects, and open the first call for community‑proposed projects.
