York University has been awarded CAD $2.5 million by the Social Sciences and Humanities Research Council of Canada (SSHRC), through its Partnership Grants program, to create the **Algorithmic Justice for Refugees Research Network (AJRRN)**.

Led by the [Refugee Law Lab](https://refugeelab.ca) at Osgoode Hall Law School, the network brings together universities, legal and advocacy organizations, technology and digital‑rights groups, and researchers with lived experience of displacement in Canada and around the world. Over the coming years it will investigate the impact of artificial intelligence and other digital technologies used in border control on refugees and other people on the move, and develop open‑source, rights‑enhancing alternatives.

The network is directed by Sean Rehaag (Osgoode Hall Law School; Director, Refugee Law Lab), with Petra Molnar (Associate Director, Refugee Law Lab) leading its human‑rights stream.
